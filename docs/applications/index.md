## Orica Geosolutions Real-World Case Studies & Benchmark Projects

The following engineering case studies represent verified field deployments of Orica Digital Solutions & Geosolutions technologies across mining, heavy civil, dam safety, and urban infrastructure.

### 1. Mining Geotechnics & Slope Stability (Applications 8, 14, 15)

#### Case Study 1.1: Early-Warning Highwall Failure Detection — PT Borneo Indobara (BIB), Indonesia
- **Brand & Technology**: **GroundProbe** SSR-XT Slope Stability Radar & Geotechnical Support Services (GSS).
- **Project Setting**: Large-scale open pit coal mine operating in equatorial tropical conditions characterized by extreme rainfall, rapid pore pressure spikes, and complex interbedded claystone/sandstone strata.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Deep-seated bedding plane shear displacements capable of triggering sudden multi-bench highwall collapses within hours of torrential monsoonal downpours.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Continuous 24/7 sub-millimeter Doppler radar scanning across active pit walls. GroundProbe's Geotechnical Support Services (GSS) provided remote monitoring oversight with calibrated inverse-velocity threshold alarms.
- **Engineering Outcome**: The SSR-XT detected non-linear accelerating displacement 18 hours prior to an impending slope collapse. The automated alarm trigger enabled the mine operations team to evacuate 3 excavators, 12 haul trucks, and all personnel from the pit floor before a 120,000 $\text{m}^3$ highwall failure occurred. Zero injuries and zero equipment damage were sustained.

#### Case Study 1.2: Atmospheric Correction in High-Altitude Mining — Bingham Canyon Mine, Utah, USA
- **Brand & Technology**: **GroundProbe** SSR-Omni Real-Time 360° Radar with Precision Atmospherics.
- **Project Setting**: One of the world's deepest open-pit copper mines (depth exceeding 1.2 km, rim-to-rim width ~4 km), subjected to severe alpine thermal inversions, blizzards, and turbulent updrafts.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Atmospheric moisture and temperature gradients distort radar phase returns, creating severe atmospheric phase screens (APS) that can mask real ground motion or generate false alarms.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Implementation of the GroundProbe SSR-Omni with advanced *Precision Atmospherics* processing, scanning the entire 360-degree pit perimeter every few minutes to mathematically decouple atmospheric phase shifts from true rock mass deformations.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Reliable sub-millimeter displacement detection throughout blinding blizzards and extreme diurnal thermal swings, ensuring safe, continuous highwall mining operations without false evacuation halts.

#### Case Study 1.3: Multi-Mine Geotechnical Fleet Management — PT Pamapersada Nusantara (PAMA), Indonesia
- **Brand & Technology**: **GroundProbe** Multi-Radar Fleet Management & Enterprise Telemetry.
- **Project Setting**: Indonesia's largest mining contractor managing heavy earthmoving across 6 massive open-cut coal operations across Kalimantan and Sumatra.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Managing heterogeneous slope hazard data across 30+ radar systems with variable geotechnical skill levels across disparate sites.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Enterprise-wide standardization of radar operating protocols, unified alarm escalation matrices, and cloud-synchronized real-time visualization dashboards.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Established standardized, auditable trigger action response plans (TARPs) across all mine concessions, reducing false-positive downtime by over 35% while maintaining an unblemished slope safety record.

### 2. Tailings Storage Facilities & Water Dams (Applications 6, 7)

#### Case Study 2.1: Emergency Breach Response & Rescue Envelope — Herculano Mineração, Brazil
- **Brand & Technology**: **GroundProbe** SSR-XT Rapid-Deployment Radar.
- **Project Setting**: A catastrophic tailings impoundment failure requiring immediate emergency search, rescue, and remediation operations in the downstream mudflow zone.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Emergency personnel and heavy machinery were exposed to secondary failures of the saturated residual headscarp and unstable tailings embankments.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Within 48 hours of the event, GroundProbe mobilized a self-contained trailer-mounted SSR-XT radar to establish an active geotechnical safety perimeter, tracking residual scarp displacement at sub-millimeter resolution.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Provided real-time, live safety alarms for hundreds of first responders and heavy equipment operators, pinpointing micrometric creep zones ($< 0.1\text{ mm/hr}$) and preventing casualties from secondary slope slumps during recovery operations.

#### Case Study 2.2: Retrospective InSAR Pre-Failure Analysis — Xe-Pian Xe-Namnoy Saddle Dam D, Laos
- **Brand & Technology**: **3vGeomatics** Sentinel-1 Retrospective InSAR Analytics.
- **Project Setting**: A major hydroelectric project in southern Laos where Saddle Dam D breached catastrophically during heavy monsoonal inflows in July 2018.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Identifying whether the auxiliary earthfill dam had exhibited unrecorded precursory deformation or internal foundation settlement prior to breaching.
- **Solution & Deployment**: 3vGeomatics processed multi-year archived Sentinel-1 C-band synthetic aperture radar imagery over the dam alignment, applying persistent scatterer interferometry (PS-InSAR) across the structure's lifecycle.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Revealed progressive differential crest subsidence and downstream slope bulging occurring months prior to the failure event. The study proved that continuous satellite InSAR surveillance can serve as a vital macro-level early detection layer for remote dams and tailings facilities.

#### Case Study 2.3: Climate-Induced Levee Embankment Deterioration — Warden Levee / ACHILLES Project, UK
- **Brand & Technology**: **RST Instruments** Vibrating Wire Piezometers, Soil Moisture Probes & Tiltmeters.
- **Project Setting**: Full-scale experimental flood embankment subject to extreme cyclic meteorological drying and wetting cycles under the UK National Infrastructure Research Consortium (ACHILLES).
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Measuring the progressive loss of shear strength and suction-driven cracking in high-plasticity clay fill embankments during prolonged droughts followed by intense storm wetting.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Automated sensor arrays featuring RST vibrating wire piezometers installed at multiple depths, capacitive matric suction sensors, and surface tiltmeter arrays linked to ultra-low-power dataloggers.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Provided high-frequency pore pressure and soil moisture boundary profiles, capturing the exact pore pressure response and strain localization that precedes progressive embankment slope failure.

### 3. Deep Excavations, Basements & High-Rise Foundations (Applications 1, 3, 11)

#### Case Study 3.1: Ultra-Deep Excavation Shoring Deflection — "The One", Toronto, Canada
- **Brand & Technology**: **Measurand** ShapeArray™ (SAAV).
- **Project Setting**: Construction of Canada's tallest architectural skyscraper (85 storeys, 308 m) requiring an 8-level underground basement excavation directly adjacent to high-traffic urban arteries and century-old brick masonry structures.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Strict municipal regulations imposed a maximum allowable lateral retaining wall deflection limit of only 6 mm to avoid damaging utility vaults and adjacent transit infrastructure.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Vertical ShapeArray (SAAV) chains installed directly inside soldier piles and perimeter caissons. The flexible SAA segments streamed continuous, automated 3D lateral displacement profiles from ground level down to the bedrock anchor zone.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Real-time displacement curves were transmitted directly to the geotechnical design team every 15 minutes, allowing adaptive adjustment of tieback anchor prestressing. Retaining wall deflections were held within allowable tolerances throughout all excavation stages.

#### Case Study 3.2: Multi-Tiered Excavation in Glacial Clays — New Westminster, BC, Canada
- **Brand & Technology**: **RST Instruments** Digital MEMS In-Place Inclinometers (IPI) & Vibrating Wire Load Cells.
- **Project Setting**: Deep multi-level residential tower excavation in overconsolidated glaciomarine clays bounded by a major commuter highway and rail corridor.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Rapid variations in ground anchor lock-off loads due to clay creep and fluctuating perched groundwater tables.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Biaxial digital MEMS in-place inclinometer strings coupled with RST vibrating wire center-hole load cells installed on multi-strand ground anchors, logged continuously by solar-powered wireless data hubs.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Automated load cell data confirmed stable load redistribution across anchor rows during bulk excavation. Eliminated the labor-intensive delays and safety risks of manual inclinometer probing in high-traffic work zones.

#### Case Study 3.3: High-Rise Shoring Adjacent to Transit SkyTrain — Gilmore Place, Burnaby, BC, Canada
- **Brand & Technology**: **RST Instruments** Strain Gauges & **Measurand** ShapeArray.
- **Project Setting**: Massive multi-tower commercial and residential complex featuring a 5-level basement excavation located mere meters from elevated SkyTrain guideway column footings.
- **Monitoring Challenge**: Transit authorities mandated continuous, zero-tolerance movement verification of elevated guideway columns and adjacent shoring walls.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Combined array of Measurand vertical ShapeArrays along the shoring perimeter, RST vibrating wire strain gauges on structural steel struts, and RST borehole tiltmeters on transit foundation bents.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Delivered 24/7 automated deformation data into an engineering web portal, proving column lateral displacements remained well under the 3 mm transit safety trigger threshold.

### 5. Rail Corridors, Highways & Regional Lifelines (Applications 4, 5, 10, 18)

- **Engineering Outcome**: Mapped localized subsidence bowls up to 25 cm/year, pinpointed vulnerable canal reaches for targeted concrete lining raises, and directed maintenance capital to the highest-risk sections before hydraulic capacity failed.

- **Monitoring Challenge**: Quantifying dynamic transient track deflections under high-speed wheel passes and cumulative plastic deformation within the sub-ballast layers.
- **Solution & Deployment**: Horizontal ShapeArray (SAAH) chains embedded beneath the ballast and sub-ballast layers, recording cross-sectional deflection profiles at 200 Hz dynamic sampling.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Captured localized tie-ballast gap formation, shoulder lateral spreading, and permanent subgrade plastic strain, validating high-performance geosynthetic ballast reinforcement designs.

#### Case Study 5.3: Soft Ground Highway Interchange Embankment — Dunkettle Interchange, Cork, Ireland

- **Solution & Deployment**: Deep borehole magnetic extensometers measuring settlement across multiple subsurface soil horizons, coupled with high-sensitivity RST vibrating wire piezometers to monitor excess pore water pressure dissipation.
- **Engineering Outcome**: Settlement and piezometric dissipation curves enabled exact execution of Asaoka consolidation predictions, ensuring 95% primary consolidation settlement was achieved prior to final asphalt surfacing.

#### Case Study 5.4: Remote Alpine Mountain Rockfall Monitoring — Highway 99, Lillooet, BC, Canada
- **Brand & Technology**: **RST Instruments** Vibrating Wire Load Cells, Crackmeters & Satellite Hubs.
- **Project Setting**: Rugged canyon highway beneath towering fractured rock bluffs subject to freeze-thaw rockfall hazard and snow avalanches, without cellular reception.
