# Rock Mechanics and Tunnelling Engineering

!!! abstract "Session Summary"
    Session 8 of *GeoVadis (GAIC 2025)* showcases state-of-the-art advances in rock mass characterization, mechanized shield tunneling (TBM), underground carbon storage, and rockfall risk mitigation. Core contributions examine seismic ahead-of-face geophysics, 3D numerical modeling of multi-tunnel interactions, ground volume loss back-analysis, hydro-mechanical coupling in carbon sequestration, and IoT-enabled early warning networks.

---

## 1. Seismic Ahead-of-Face Geophysics for Tunnels (S.C. Chian, Y.Z. Tan, Y.E. Li, C.H.A. Cheng)

### Preventing Underground Geological Disasters
Unexpected encounters with fault shear zones, karst caverns, or high-pressure water inflows during tunnel excavation cause catastrophic collapses and project delays. Chian et al. demonstrate advanced **Tunnel Seismic Prediction (TSP)** systems:
- **Wave Propagation & Reflection:** High-frequency seismic waves generated by micro-blasts or pneumatic impacts at the sidewall reflect off acoustic impedance boundaries ahead of the tunnel face.
- **Look-Ahead Range:** Triaxial accelerometer sensors mounted on the tunnel lining process P- and S-wave reflection hodograms, accurately imaging geological discontinuities and fracture zones up to $100\,\text{m} - 150\,\text{m}$ in advance of the excavation face.
- **Safety Protocol:** Real-time geophysical inversions alert the excavation team to perform exploratory probe drilling and advance consolidation grouting before advancing into water-bearing fault zones.

## 2. 3D Numerical Simulation of Mechanized TBM Tunneling (J. Agarwal, G. Garg, et al.)

### Multi-Stage Shield Tunneling in Layered Strata
Using MIDAS GTS-NX, Agarwal and colleagues perform fully coupled 3D finite element simulations of Earth Pressure Balance (EPB) TBM advancement through stratified sands and stiff clays:
- **Face Support Pressure:** Face pressure is calibrated to counterbalance in-situ horizontal earth pressure and pore water pressure ($p_{face} = K_0 \sigma'_v + u_w$), preventing face extrusion and catastrophic surface sinkholes.
- **Grouting Annulus & Lining Jacking:** The simulation models pressurized tail-void grout injection ($p_{grout} \approx 200 - 300\,\text{kPa}$), TBM shield taper friction, and hydraulic ram jacking thrusts on precast segment rings.
- **Transverse Settlement Trough:** Ground surface settlement profiles conform closely to Peck's Gaussian error distribution:
  $$S(x) = S_{max} \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{x^2}{2 i^2} \right)$$
  where the trough width parameter $i = K \cdot z_0$ is accurately captured across alternating clay-sand strata.

## 3. Structural Responses of Existing Tunnels to New Adjacent Bores (J.Q. Lin, J.H. Zhang, X.S. Chen, et al.)

### Proximity Tunnelling in High-Density Urban Space
Excavating twin or intersecting transit tunnels induces asymmetrical stress relief around pre-existing operational tunnels:
- **Deformation Mechanisms:** Stress redistribution causes diametrical distortion (horizontal elongation and vertical squatting) of pre-existing segmental rings.
- **Joint Opening & Bolt Tension:** Differential shearing between segment rings induces circumferential joint opening and sharp tensile stress spikes in radial connecting bolts.
- **Mitigation Protocols:** Pre-grouting the soil pillar between tunnel bores and adjusting advance rates limit existing tunnel convergence within strict rail operation thresholds ($< 5\,\text{mm}$).

## 4. Hydro-Mechanical Coupling in $\text{CO}_2$ Saline Aquifer Sequestration (S.M. Chiang, M.C. Weng, H.K. Le, et al.)

### Caprock Integrity & Fault Reactivation
Supercritical carbon dioxide ($\text{scCO}_2$) injection into deep saline aquifers ($> 1,000\,\text{m}$ depth) increases reservoir pore pressure, altering the local stress field:
- **Coupled Seepage-Stress Analysis:** Chiang et al. formulate hydro-mechanically coupled finite element models governing two-phase fluid flow and porous rock deformability.
- **Fault Slip Potential:** Injection-induced pore pressure reduces effective normal stress ($\sigma'_n$) on bounding faults, potentially triggering fault reactivation and seismic events if injection pressure exceeds critical threshold bounds:
  $$\tau \le c' + (\sigma_n - u_{pore})\tan\phi'$$
- **Caprock Sealing Verification:** The study quantifies maximum sustainable injection rates that maintain caprock tensile stresses below rock tensile strength ($f_t$), ensuring zero upward $\text{CO}_2$ leakage.

## 5. IoT-Based Real-Time Rockfall & Debris Flow Alert Systems (R. Budhbhatti, R.R. Mahajan, D. Paldino)

### Edge-Computing Early Warning Networks
Highway and rail corridors traversing steep Himalayan and alpine rock slopes face sudden rockfall impacts and rapid debris flows. Budhbhatti et al. present a field-deployed IoT monitoring system:
- **Sensor Node Deployment:** Micro-electro-mechanical (MEMS) tiltmeters and load cells installed on flexible ring-net barriers track dynamic impact impulses ($E > 500\,\text{kJ}$).
- **Automated Alarm Protocols (TARP):** Wireless LoRaWAN gateways transmit telemetry to cloud servers; when barrier cable tension or post tilt rates exceed Level 3 Trigger Action Response Plan (TARP) thresholds, automated signals trigger trackside warning lights to halt approaching trains.
