## Overview and Focus of Volume 2

While Volume 1 focused on plenary foundations, AI geomechanics, cold/environmental frontiers, earthquake dynamics, deep excavations, and geosynthetics, **Volume 2** advances applied geotechnical problem-solving across five vital infrastructure themes:

- **Ground Improvement and Chemical Stabilisation:** Innovative methods to counteract subgrade fluidization under cyclic heavy-haul rail traffic, geopolymer-stabilized soil columns, carbon nanotube additives for liquefaction suppression, biopolymer treatment (xanthan gum), and non-linear vertical drain preloading consolidation.
- **Geotechnical Practice, Risk, and Site Characterisation:** Modern site investigation techniques including time-lapse Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT) for internal seepage tracking, multichannel analysis of surface waves (MASW), Dilatometer Test (DMT) dissipation for overconsolidation ratio ($OCR$), Building Information Modelling (BIM) workflows, and critical forensic evaluation of Standard Penetration Test (SPT) practices.
- **Rock Mechanics and Underground Tunnelling:** Numerical 3D TBM tunneling simulation through stratified urban geology, advanced seismic look-ahead geophysical probing, geological carbon sequestration in saline aquifers, ground subsidence mitigation under static/seismic loading, and IoT-driven early warning networks for rockfall hazards.
- **Slope Stability, Forensic Landslides, and Early Warning:** Forensic model calibration of open-pit mine wall failures, rainfall-induced landslide thresholds using the TRIGRS and Soil-Water Index (SWI) frameworks, random finite element analysis (RFEM) incorporating spatial permeability heterogeneity, and helical soil nail reinforcement.
- **Transportation Geotechnics:** Dynamic train-track-subgrade interaction, waste conveyor-belt rubber grids for railway ballast cushioning, geocell-reinforced expansive subgrades in flexible pavements, lightweight EPS geofoam highway embankments on ultra-soft clay, and pavement structural responses to electric vehicle fleet axle loads.

## Primary Monitoring Instrumentation Alignment

The field implementations and laboratory research in Volume 2 interface directly with core geotechnical sensing technologies:
- **Piezometers (Áp kế):** Vibrating wire piezometers tracking rapid pore pressure generation during cyclic heavy rail passes, vacuum preloading dissipation, and infiltration wetting front movements.
- **Inclinometers & Extensometers (Ống đo nghiêng & Thiết bị đo biến dạng sâu):** Vertical and lateral displacement profiles behind back-to-back MSE walls, TBM tunnel subsidence troughs, and open-pit mine slopes.
- **Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT) & Geophysical Arrays:** Non-destructive imaging of internal seepage channels, piping flaws, and time-dependent moisture infiltration fronts in earth embankments.
- **Strain Gauges & Sister Bars (Cảm biến đo biến dạng & Sister Bar):** Structural strain and load transfer monitoring in helical soil nails, tunnel shotcrete linings, and geosynthetic reinforcing strips.
- **Load Cells (Cảm biến đo tải trọng):** Prestress retention monitoring in rock bolts, soil anchors, and railway sleeper ballast load cells.
