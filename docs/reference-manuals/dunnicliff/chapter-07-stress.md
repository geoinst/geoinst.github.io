# Chapter 7 — Measurement of Stress

## 7.1 Two different stress problems

Stress monitoring splits into two distinct tasks:

1. **Total stress in soil** — the pressure exerted by soil or a structure on a
   plane (e.g., against a retaining wall, under a footing).
2. **Stress change in rock** — the change in the in-situ stress field caused by
   excavation or loading.

The instruments and methods differ sharply.

## 7.2 Total stress in soil — earth pressure cells

**Earth pressure cells** measure the total normal stress acting on a plane:

- **Hydraulic** cells — liquid-filled, robust, good for total pressure.
- **Vibrating-wire** cells — automated, common in walls and embankments.
- **Pneumatic** cells — no power needed.
- **Contact stress cells** — measure pressure at a soil–structure interface.

Installation must place the cell's sensing face flush with the surface it
monitors, with minimal disturbance, or the reading is biased.

## 7.3 Stress change in rock

Rock stress changes are measured by instruments placed in boreholes:

- **Borehole pressure cells / stressmeters** — monitor stress change around
  openings.
- **Flat jacks** — measure stress relief in a slot.
- **Overcoring** — a stress-relief method that, with strain measurement,
  back-calculates the original in-situ stress state.

## 7.4 Interpretation cautions

Stress measurements are sensitive to installation disturbance and to the
stiffness mismatch between cell and ground. Read them as **relative change** more
reliably than as absolute values, and correlate with deformation (Chapter 8).

!!! warning "Stiffness mismatch"
    A cell much stiffer than the surrounding soil over-reads; much softer,
    under-reads. Match the cell to the medium, or interpret with that bias in
    mind.

## 7.5 Key takeaways

- Total stress in soil and stress change in rock need different instruments.
- Earth pressure cells need flush, undisturbed installation.
- Rock stress is best read as change, via cells, flat jacks, or overcoring.
- Account for stiffness mismatch when interpreting.
