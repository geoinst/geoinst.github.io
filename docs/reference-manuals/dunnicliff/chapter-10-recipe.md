# Chapter 10 — A Recipe for Reliability

## 10.1 Reliability is not accidental

Dunnicliff's "recipe for reliability" distills the ingredients of monitoring
that actually produces trustworthy data. Miss an ingredient and the result
suffers. The ingredients fall into themes:

## 10.2 The ingredient themes

- **Selection** — the right instrument for the question and environment.
- **Procurement** — specify performance, calibration, and spares.
- **Installation** — skilled, documented, verified.
- **Calibration** — factory and field, with traceability.
- **Reading** — competent people, correct frequency, validated.
- **Maintenance** — scheduled checks and prompt repair.
- **Data handling** — validated, archived, retrievable.
- **People** — trained, accountable, empowered to report bad news.

## 10.3 The 25 links, in spirit

The recipe echoes the **chain of 25 links** (Chapter 14): from defining the need
through to a reading that informs a decision, every step must hold. A program is
only as strong as its weakest link — usually installation or reading, not the
sensor.

## 10.4 Where programs fail

The recurring weak links are:

- Instruments installed but never saturated, grouted, or referenced correctly.
- Readings taken but never validated or plotted.
- Data filed and forgotten, with no one interpreting trend.
- Personnel afraid or unempowered to raise concerns.

## 10.5 Making reliability routine

- Write the recipe into the project's monitoring plan.
- Assign clear ownership of each ingredient.
- Audit the chain periodically — especially after staff or contractor changes.

!!! tip "Treat the recipe as a checklist"
    Convert the ingredient themes into a living checklist reviewed at each
    project milestone. A checked checklist is evidence the chain is intact.

## 10.6 Key takeaways

- Reliability has identifiable ingredients; missing any weakens the result.
- Weak links are usually installation, reading, and interpretation — not sensors.
- Empowered, competent people are an ingredient, not overhead.
- Review the recipe at every milestone.
