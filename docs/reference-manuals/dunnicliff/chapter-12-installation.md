# Chapter 12 — Installation of Instruments

## 12.1 Installation makes or breaks the data

The best-specified instrument yields useless data if installed poorly.
Installation is where most monitoring programs win or lose.

## 12.2 Drilling and boreholes

- Avoid **smearing** the borehole wall (which seals off the zone you want to
  measure) by using appropriate bits and methods.
- Keep the borehole open and clean at the measurement zone.
- Protect against collapse in软 soils.

## 12.3 Grouting, casing, and sealing

- **Filter packs** around piezometers and inclinometer anchors must be clean and
  correctly graded.
- **Sealing (grout/cutoff)** prevents water from short-circuiting down the
  borehole to a lower zone — a classic source of wrong readings.
- **Casing** for inclinometers must be vertical (or known orientation) and fully
  grouted for good coupling to the ground.

## 12.4 Tubing and cable routing

- Protect pneumatic tubing and sensor cables from damage, kinking, water, and
  rodents.
- Label and route to accessible, sheltered readout points.
- Keep cable lengths within the instrument's specified limits.

## 12.5 Installation records

For every instrument, record permanently:

- Location, depth, orientation, and datum.
- Strata encountered and filter-pack / seal details.
- As-installed photos and as-found calibration.
- Date and personnel.

These records are essential for interpretation and for replacing the instrument
later.

## 12.6 Personnel qualifications

Installation is skilled work. Specify and verify the competence of those
drilling, grouting, and placing instruments.

!!! danger "Short-circuiting destroys a piezometer"
    A single uncorrected path for water down a borehole can make a deep
    piezometer read a shallow zone. Seal meticulously; verify with a standpipe
    or observation well.

## 12.7 Key takeaways

- Installation quality determines data quality more than sensor grade.
- Prevent smearing and short-circuiting; grout and seal meticulously.
- Protect and label tubing and cables.
- Keep permanent, detailed installation records.
