# Chapter 14 — Applications & The Chain of Success

## 14.1 Putting it together

The preceding chapters are ingredients. This chapter shows how they combine for
common project types and closes with Dunnicliff's unifying idea — the chain of
25 links.

## 14.2 Braced excavations

Typical instruments: **strut load cells**, **wall-deflection inclinometers**,
**ground-movement survey**, and **piezometers**. The goal is to confirm the wall
and struts stay within allowable movement and pressure.

## 14.3 Embankments on soft ground

**Settlement** (embankment and foundation), **pore pressure** (to check
consolidation and stability), and **lateral spread** are monitored to confirm
the embankment is stabilizing as predicted.

## 14.4 Embankment dams

**Pore pressure** (Chapter 6), **deformation** (Chapter 8), **seepage
quantification**, and — increasingly — **automated systems** confirm safe
performance. See also the [Monitoring Dam Performance](../monitoring-dam-performance/index.md)
and [Tailings Dam Safety](../tailings-dam-safety/index.md) books.

## 14.5 Excavated and natural slopes

**Inclinometers** (deep shear), **piezometers** (pore pressure), **tiltmeters**,
and **surface survey** track stability; early movement triggers evaluation.

## 14.6 Underground excavations

**Convergence** (Chapter 8), **rock-bolt load cells** (Chapter 9), **crown
displacement**, and **groundwater** monitor support performance and warn of
instability.

## 14.7 Driven piles and drilled shafts

**Load cells**, **telltales**, and **strain gages** support static load tests
and integrity assessment; downdrag and tip load are among the monitored
quantities.

## 14.8 The chain with 25 links

Dunnicliff's enduring metaphor: monitoring success is a **chain of 25 links**
from defining the project's need to a reading that informs a decision. The links
span planning, procurement, installation, calibration, reading, maintenance, data
handling, and people. **Break any link and the chain fails.** The recipe
(Chapter 10) is the chain made operational.

## 14.9 Key takeaways

- Instruments combine differently for each project type.
- Dams, slopes, excavations, tunnels, and piles each have a characteristic set.
- The 25-link chain unifies the discipline: any broken link defeats the purpose.
- People, process, and hardware are all links in that chain.
