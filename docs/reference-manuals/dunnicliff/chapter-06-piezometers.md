# Chapter 6 — Measurement of Groundwater Pressure

## 6.1 Pore pressure is everywhere important

Groundwater (pore) pressure governs effective stress, slope stability,
settlement, and liquefaction. Measuring it is one of the most common and most
consequential monitoring tasks.

## 6.2 Piezometer types

| Type | Principle | Best for |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Vibrating-wire (VW)** | Frequency change of a tensioned wire | Long-term automated monitoring; robust |
| **Pneumatic** | Gas pressure balances water pressure | Remote, no power |
| **Standpipe (open)** | Water level in a pipe | Simple, direct, slow to equilibrate |
| **Hydraulic (twin-tube)** | Liquid-column balance | Multiple points, established method |

## 6.3 The saturation imperative

A VW or pneumatic piezometer must be **fully saturated** — free of air in the
porous filter and connecting tube — or it reads nonsense. The saturation
procedure (de-airing, filling, and checking) is the single most neglected step
in field installation. An unsaturated piezometer is a silent gap in the program.

## 6.4 Installation in soil, rock, and boreholes

- **Soil:** careful borehole drilling to avoid smearing the surrounding soil;
  filter pack and sealing to prevent short-circuiting.
- **Rock:** packer or grouted installations to isolate the measurement zone.
- **Multi-level (MPBX):** several sensors in one borehole to profile pressure
  with depth.

## 6.5 Reading and interpretation

Readings convert to **piezometric head**. Interpret against:

- **Phreatic surface** location and change.
- **Pore-pressure response** to rainfall, pond level, or loading.
- **Excess pressure** after seismic or construction events.

## 6.6 Pitfalls

- Air ingress / loss of saturation → drifting readings.
- Borehole short-circuiting → reads the wrong zone.
- Cabling damage → the most common automated failure.

!!! tip "Cross-check with a standpipe"
    Where pore pressure is critical, a simple open standpipe alongside VW
    sensors provides an independent, foolproof check that the automated data is
    physically sensible.

## 6.7 Key takeaways

- Pore pressure governs effective stress and stability.
- Saturate every piezometer; verify it.
- Isolate the measurement zone; prevent short-circuiting.
- Standpipes offer a cheap independent cross-check.
