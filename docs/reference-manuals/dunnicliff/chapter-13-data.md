# Chapter 13 — Data Collection, Processing & Interpretation

## 13.1 Data only matters if it is used

A shelf of读数 logs is not monitoring. This chapter covers turning raw readings
into decisions.

## 13.2 Initial readings and baseline

- Take **initial (baseline) readings** after installation and after settlement
  stabilizes.
- Multiple initial readings reduce the effect of transient disturbance.
- The baseline is the reference against which all future change is judged.

## 13.3 Reading frequency

Frequency depends on:

- Parameter dynamics (fast-moving during construction; slow in steady operation).
- Life stage and risk category.
- Event-driven increases after storms, earthquakes, or anomalies.

Too infrequent misses rapid change; too frequent wastes effort. Tune it.

## 13.4 Automated systems

Automated data acquisition supports high frequency, continuous coverage, and
alarms — essential where movement can accelerate quickly. It demands the same
validation and maintenance discipline as manual reading.

## 13.5 Validation and processing

Before use, data is checked for plausibility, continuity, and calibration drift,
and corrected for temperature and environmental effects. Gaps are documented,
not hidden.

## 13.6 Presentation and interpretation

Effective presentation reveals signal:

- **Time-series** against driving variables (load, rainfall, season).
- **Cross-plots** (e.g., movement vs. pore pressure).
- **Profiles** with depth or distance.

Interpretation asks: does measured behavior match expected? A deviation triggers
evaluation, not panic — and acceleration is the earliest warning.

!!! tip "Plot against the driver"
    A pore-pressure time-series alone is hard to read; plotted against reservoir
    or rainfall level, the relationship — and any anomaly — becomes obvious.

## 13.7 Key takeaways

- Establish baseline readings; judge change against them.
- Tune frequency to dynamics, life stage, and risk.
- Validate, correct, and document gaps before interpreting.
- Plot against driving variables to reveal signal.
