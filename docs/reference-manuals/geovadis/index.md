## Overview and Significance

*GeoVadis: The Future of Geotechnical Engineering (Volume 1)* compiles state-of-the-art research, cutting-edge engineering methodologies, and real-world case histories from the **First Geotech Asia International Conference (GAIC 2025)** held under the auspices of the Asian technical committees of the International Society for Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering (ISSMGE).

The compendium captures the transformative shifts shaping 21st-century geotechnics across 19 technical themes:
- **Digital Transformation & Computational Geomechanics:** Application of Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), Deep Neural Networks (DNN), Discrete Element Modeling (DEM), and optimized machine learning algorithms to granular mechanics, cyclic degradation, compaction, and liquefaction assessment.
- **Climate Resilience & Environmental Geotechnics:** The mechanical and hydrological impact of freeze-thaw cycles, desiccation cracking, bentonite buffer performance under extreme chemical and thermal gradients, and sustainable geothermal energy storage in foundation geo-structures.
- **Seismic Hazard & Dynamic Soil-Structure Interaction:** Advanced seismic risk assessment, small-strain shear modulus degradation, ground vibration isolation using sand-rubber mixtures, and liquefaction mitigation using green biopolymers.
- **Deep Excavations & Foundation Systems:** Centrifuge testing and field validation of soil-cement composite piles, bi-directional static load pile tests (BDSLT / Osterberg cell), diaphragm wall trench stability with enlarged panels, and deep soil mixing excavation supports.
- **Geosynthetics & Soil Reinforcement:** Micro-mechanical interface friction, pullout resistance of overlapping geogrids, geocell-reinforced structures under dynamic and hydraulic stresses, and geotextile tube dewatering for mine tailings and contaminated slurries.

## Core Instrumentation & Field Monitoring Parallels

Throughout *GeoVadis (Volume 1)*, experimental and field verification relies directly on the core geotechnical sensor systems detailed in this knowledge base:
- **Piezometers (Áp kế):** High-frequency vibrating wire piezometers for pore pressure dissipation during vacuum preloading, cyclic shear liquefaction verification, and infiltration wetting front tracking.
- **Strain Gauges & Sister Bars (Cảm biến đo biến dạng & Sister Bar):** Structural strain and bending moment measurement in bored piles, soil-cement composite piles, and diaphragm walls during deep excavation stages.
- **Load Cells (Cảm biến đo tải trọng):** Calibrated load cells for Osterberg-style bi-directional static load testing, tieback anchor prestress tracking, and plate anchor pullout evaluation.
- **Inclinometers & Extensometers (Ống đo nghiêng & Thiết bị đo biến dạng sâu):** Lateral displacement profiling behind deep excavation support systems and deep settlement monitoring in multi-layered soft marine clays.
- **Earth Pressure Cells (Hộp đo áp lực đất):** Direct total stress and dynamic earth pressure measurements against yielding rigid retaining walls and reinforced earth wall fascia panels.
