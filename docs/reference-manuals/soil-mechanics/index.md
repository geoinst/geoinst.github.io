# Soil Mechanics & Geotechnical Engineering: A Public-Domain Reference

!!! note "About this reference"
    This is an **original, open technical reference** on soil mechanics and
    geotechnical engineering, written in plain engineering language. Like the
    [Foundation Engineering](../foundation-engineering/index.md) book, it is built
    **entirely from public-domain sources** — the **FHWA NHI *Soils and Foundations
    Reference Manual*** and the **USACE Engineering Manuals**, all of which are
    **U.S. Government works in the public domain**. No copyrighted textbook is
    reproduced. It is provided for education and field use; every site is unique and
    this reference builds engineering judgment, not minimum standards. The
    underlying manuals are indexed in the [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md) AI
    assistant.

**Soil mechanics** is the science of how soil — a three-phase material of solids,
water, and air — responds to stress and water. It is the foundation of every
geotechnical calculation: bearing capacity, settlement, slope stability, earth
pressure, and seepage all rest on it. This reference covers the classical
core — composition and classification, compaction, permeability and seepage,
effective stress, consolidation, shear strength, and stiffness — plus the practical
matters of in-situ testing, parameter selection, and problem soils.

## How to use this reference

- **Read it alongside the [Foundation Engineering](../foundation-engineering/index.md)
  book** — this one supplies the soil parameters and mechanics; that one applies
  them to foundations.
- **Use it with the monitoring books.** Pore pressure (Chapter 7) and shear strength
  (Chapter 10) are the physics behind the [Dunnicliff](../dunnicliff/index.md) and
  [FHWA](../fhwa/index.md) instrument references.
- **Ask the [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md)** for the underlying manual text.

> **Parameters are not truth.** Every soil parameter carries uncertainty; good
> engineering bounds it and designs for the range, not a single number.
