# CFEM 2022 — Geotechnical Instrumentation and Monitoring

!!! note "About this reference"
    This is an **original, open digest** of **Chapter 25 — *Geotechnical
    Instrumentation and Monitoring*** from the **Canadian Foundation Engineering
    Manual (CFEM), 4th Edition (2022)**, authored by Pierre Choquet. It is a
    chapter-level summary with an **elaborated reference section** — it is **not**
    a reproduction of the copyrighted source text. The CFEM is published by the
    Canadian Geotechnical Society; the original chapter and its figures remain the
    copyright of the author, the publisher and the credited suppliers. This digest
    is provided for education and orientation; consult the original for design use.

The **Canadian Foundation Engineering Manual (CFEM)** is the Canadian
geotechnical profession's principal reference work. The 4th Edition (2022) added a
full chapter on instrumentation — **Chapter 25, *Geotechnical Instrumentation and
Monitoring*** — written by **Pierre Choquet, Dr.-Eng., P. Eng.**, a recognised
authority on vibrating-wire instrumentation and automated data acquisition.

At **48 pages**, Chapter 25 is a compact, unusually *practical* survey of the whole
field: what to measure, with what, how to read it, and how to read it *automatically*.
Its distinguishing strength is the breadth of its **reference apparatus** — the
structured reading list in **§25.2, *Geotechnical Instrumentation and Monitoring
Information Sources***, backed by a 45-entry reference list. That apparatus is the
reason this page exists: it is a ready-made map of the authoritative standards,
textbooks, and regulatory manuals behind modern monitoring practice, and it is
**elaborated in full** on the chapter page below.

## Why this chapter matters in this knowledge base

Chapter 25 is the **standards-and-practice backbone** the rest of this library
leans on:

- Its **information-sources list** (§25.2) names exactly the standards (ASTM, ISO,
  DIN), textbooks (Dunnicliff "Red Book", De Rubertis, Sharon & Eberhardt, Hoek),
  and **regulation manuals** (USACE, Reclamation, FERC, ICOLD) used across this site.
- Its **automated data-acquisition** material (§25.15–25.16) maps directly onto the
  site's vendor-neutral [ADAQS six-layer architecture](../../vi/adaqs/index.md).
- Its **instrument-by-instrument** sections cross-reference the site's
  [Dunnicliff](../dunnicliff/index.md) and [FHWA](../fhwa/index.md) digests.

## How to use this reference

- **Start with the [chapter page](chapter-25-instrumentation-monitoring.md)** and
  its reference table — it is the most reusable part of the chapter.
- Use the **grouped reference table** to find the governing standard or manual for
  a given instrument.
- Follow the cross-links into the [Dunnicliff](../dunnicliff/index.md),
  [FHWA](../fhwa/index.md), [Monitoring Dam Performance](../monitoring-dam-performance/index.md)
  and [Tailings Dam Safety](../tailings-dam-safety/index.md) digests for depth.
- Ask the [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md) to pull specifics from the wider corpus.

> **Safety and currency.** Standards are revised on their own cycles (ISO 18674,
> ASTM D18.23, DIN 4150 among them). Always verify the **current edition** of any
> standard or manual before relying on it for a design or regulatory submission.
