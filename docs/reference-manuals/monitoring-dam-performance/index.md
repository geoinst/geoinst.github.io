# Monitoring Dam Performance: Instrumentation and Measurements

!!! note "About this reference"
    This is an **original, open technical reference** on dam-performance monitoring,
    written in plain engineering language and adapted from the structure and subject
    scope of *Monitoring Dam Performance: Instrumentation and Measurements* — **ASCE
    Manual of Practice No. 135 (2019)**, prepared by the ASCE/EWRI Task Committee on
    Instrumentation and Monitoring of Dams and Levees. It is **not** a reproduction of
    the copyrighted ASCE text. It is provided for education and field use, and every
    dam is unique: the guidance here is meant to build engineering judgment, not to
    set minimum standards.

**Dam safety** depends on knowing how a dam actually behaves. Monitoring is the
discipline of measuring that behavior — through both human observation and
instruments — and turning those measurements into timely, defensible decisions.
This reference walks through the full lifecycle of a monitoring program: from
defining what could go wrong, to selecting and installing instruments, to
acquiring, evaluating, and acting on data.

## A note on units and terminology

Both SI and US customary units appear in practice; this reference uses the unit set
most common to each topic and states conversions where they matter. "Performance"
means the observed behavior of the dam compared with its expected behavior.

> **Safety first.** Instrument data supports, but never replaces, qualified
> visual inspection and the engineering judgment of a qualified dam engineer or
> the owner's dam safety program.
