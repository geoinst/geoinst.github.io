# Geotechnical Engineer's Handbook (Trần Văn Việt)

!!! note "About this reference"
    This is an **original technical digest** written for this knowledge base,
    following the subject scope and structure of *Cẩm nang dùng cho kỹ sư địa kỹ
    thuật* (*Geotechnical Engineer's Handbook*) by **Trần Văn Việt**, published by
    **Nhà xuất bản Xây dựng** (Construction Publishing House). The source book is
    **copyrighted**: this page is **not** a reproduction of its text — it
    summarises the key concepts and systematises the Vietnamese professional
    terminology, with pointers back to the original. It is provided for education
    and reference; every project is unique.

*Cẩm nang dùng cho kỹ sư địa kỹ thuật* is a Vietnamese practitioner's handbook for
civil engineers. It consolidates geotechnical knowledge and experience gathered from
infrastructure projects in Vietnam and cross-checks it against the codes and standards
of developed countries in Europe and America. It spans the full geotechnical workflow
in two blocks:

- **Soil investigation** (Chapters I–IV): the geological environment (soil and rock),
  the engineering properties of foundation soils, groundwater, and the complete
  system of methods, techniques and equipment for investigation, exploration and
  testing.
- **Geotechnical analysis** (Chapters V–XII): shallow and deep foundation analysis,
  slope stability, retaining walls, embankments on soft ground, engineering
  hydraulics, earthquakes, and information technology.

## How to use this reference

- **Follow the workflow.** If you are investigating, start at Chapters I–IV. If you
  are designing, go straight to Chapters V–VIII for your structure type.
- **Chapter 9 is the closest to this site's focus.** Its *Monitoring and verification*
  section covers settlement monitoring, pore-pressure measurement, lateral
  displacement and total vertical stress beneath embankments. Read it alongside the
  [Dunnicliff](../dunnicliff/index.md) and [FHWA](../fhwa/index.md) manuals.
- **Use the terminology table at the end of every chapter.** It is the most valuable
  thing this digest preserves: the book's Vietnamese terms with their English
  equivalents.
- **Cross-check against the site glossary.** See the
  [Glossary](../../glossary/index.md) for the site's canonical 1-to-1 TCVN mapping.
- **Ask the [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md).** The AI assistant answers from the
  indexed corpus.

## Terminology and normalisation

The book uses the terminology common in Vietnamese practice, which sometimes differs
from the canonical TCVN mapping this site applies. This digest keeps the **book's own
terms** (for cross-reference) and adds the **canonical term** in each chapter, e.g.:

| The book says | The site's canonical TCVN term |
| --- | --- |
| "áp lực nước lỗ rỗng" | **Pore-water pressure** (*áp lực nước lỗ rỗng*) |
| "thiết bị đo áp lực nước" | **Piezometer** (*áp kế*) |
| "ứng suất hữu hiệu" | **Effective stress** (*ứng suất hữu hiệu*) |
| "mô đun nén ngang" | **Pressuremeter modulus** (*mô đun nén ngang*) |
| "thiết bị đo biến dạng sâu" | **Extensometer** (*thiết bị đo biến dạng sâu*) |
