# Foundation Engineering: A Public-Domain Reference

!!! note "About this reference"
    This is an **original, open technical reference** on foundation engineering,
    written in plain engineering language. Unlike the other books in this section,
    it is built **entirely from public-domain sources** — the **U.S. Army Corps of
    Engineers (USACE) Engineering Manuals** and the **Federal Highway
    Administration (FHWA) National Highway Institute (NHI)** geotechnical manuals,
    all of which are **U.S. Government works in the public domain**. No copyrighted
    textbook is reproduced. It is provided for education and field use; every
    project is unique and this reference is meant to build engineering judgment, not
    to set minimum standards. The underlying manuals are indexed in the
    [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md) AI assistant for deeper queries.

**Foundation engineering** is the discipline of designing the interface between a
structure and the ground: spread footings and mats, deep foundations, retaining
structures, and the earth that supports and presses against them. It rests on soil
mechanics, but its practice is governed by design manuals — and the two most
authoritative, freely available, and legally reusable bodies of guidance in the
English-speaking world are the **USACE Engineering Manuals** and the **FHWA NHI
manuals**.

This reference consolidates that public-domain guidance into a single,
chapter-by-chapter treatment: how to explore a site; how shallow foundations carry
load and settle; how deep foundations transfer load; how retaining walls, sheet
piles, and braced excavations resist earth pressure; how slopes are analysed; and
how ground improvement and instrumentation support the design.

## The public-domain sources

This reference is an independent adaptation of the scope and structure of these
**public-domain** manuals (U.S. Government works):

| Source | Title | Scope |
|--------|-------|-------|
| USACE EM 1110-1-1905 | *Geotechnical Design of Shallow Foundations on Soils* (formerly *Bearing Capacity of Soils*) | Bearing capacity of shallow and deep foundations |
| USACE EM 1110-1-1904 | *Settlement Analysis* | Elastic and consolidation settlement |
| USACE EM 1110-2-2906 | *Design of Pile Foundations* | Pile capacity, negative skin friction, lateral loads |
| USACE EM 1110-2-2502 | *Retaining and Flood Walls* | Retaining-wall design and stability |
| USACE EM 1110-2-2504 | *Design of Sheet Pile Walls* | Cantilever and anchored sheet pile walls |
| USACE EM 1110-2-1902 | *Slope Stability* | Slope stability analysis and stabilization |
| FHWA-NHI-16-009/-010 (GEC-12) | *Design and Construction of Driven Pile Foundations* | Driven piles, analysis, and construction |
| FHWA-NHI-10-024/-025 (GEC-11) | *MSE Walls and Reinforced Soil Slopes* | Mechanically stabilized earth |
| FHWA-NHI-14-007 (GEC-7) | *Soil Nail Walls* | Soil nailing design and construction |
| FHWA-IF-99-025 | *Ground Improvement Technical Summaries* | Densification, reinforcement, grouting |

> **Public domain means reusable.** USACE and FHWA manuals are works of the U.S.
> federal government and are not subject to copyright in the United States. This
> reference nevertheless presents them as an original synthesis, not a verbatim
> copy, so that it can be translated, adapted, and applied freely.

## How to use this reference

- **Start with Chapter 3 and Chapter 4** for shallow foundations, and Chapter 6–8
  for deep foundations — these are the two halves of the discipline.
- **Use it with the rest of the knowledge base.** The instrument chapters
  cross-reference the [Dunnicliff](../dunnicliff/index.md),
  [FHWA](../fhwa/index.md), and dam books for monitoring-specific detail.
- **Ask the [GTI Doctor](../../gti-doctor.md)** for the underlying manual text.

> **Design is site-specific.** No reference replaces a proper site investigation,
> and no chart replaces the judgment of a qualified geotechnical engineer.
