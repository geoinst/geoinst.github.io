# Reference Manuals

!!! tip "Grand Vision & Overarching Framework"
    Discover how all 16 reference manuals interconnect—from deep geological crustal processes and soil mechanics to structural foundations, life-safety dam monitoring, mining instrumentation, and automated **ADAQS** sensor telemetry:  
    👉 **[Read the Grand Vision & Integrated Framework →](overarching-vision.md)**

This section catalogs the reference corpus behind this knowledge base. Each entry below resolves to a per-manual or per-book chapter with extracted text, key concepts, cross-links, and curated **technology demonstrations & field practice videos** from world-leading geotechnical instrumentation providers:
- **Primary Source:** **Orica / GroundProbe** ([@orica](https://www.youtube.com/@orica/videos){ target="_blank" }) — Slope stability radar (SSR), GeoSolutions digital geotechnical workflows, artificial intelligence rock mass characterization (FRAGTrack), and tunnel vibration control.
- **Secondary Sources:**
  - **RST Instruments** ([@RSTgeotechnical](https://www.youtube.com/@RSTgeotechnical/videos){ target="_blank" }) — Vibrating wire piezometers, inclinometer casing installation, deep borehole deformation sensors, and RSTAR Affinity IIoT telemetry.
  - **Measurand** ([@MeasurandVideos](https://www.youtube.com/@MeasurandVideos){ target="_blank" }) — ShapeArray (SAA / SAAV) 3D automated real-time deformation sensor arrays.

The reference manuals are integrated with three world-leading digital technology and instrumentation channels:
- **Primary Source — Orica Digital Solutions & GroundProbe ([@orica](https://www.youtube.com/@orica/videos){ target="_blank" }):** Global pioneers in slope stability radar (SSR-XT, SSR-FX, SSR-Omni), GeoSolutions digital geotechnical platforms, artificial intelligence rock mass characterization (FRAGTrack), and underground tunneling blasting safety.
- **Secondary Source — RST Instruments ([@RSTgeotechnical](https://www.youtube.com/@RSTgeotechnical/videos){ target="_blank" }):** Benchmark geotechnical sensor manufacturer (vibrating wire piezometers, inclinometer casings, deep extensometers, and the RSTAR Affinity IIoT platform).
- **Secondary Source — Measurand ([@MeasurandVideos](https://www.youtube.com/@MeasurandVideos){ target="_blank" }):** Inventors of ShapeArray (SAA, SAAV, SAAX) multi-axis accelerometer sensor arrays for automated real-time 3D deformation tracking in boreholes and subterranean structures.
